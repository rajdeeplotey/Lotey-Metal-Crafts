The Craft Philosophy

From Raw Steel to Finished Masterpiece

True custom fabrication cannot be mass-produced in a warehouse. Here is how our workshop brings raw iron into structural art.

01

Laser Measure On-Site

Every building in Ludhiana and Himachal has unique masonry angles. Inderjit Singh personally visits the site with optical laser measurement tools to record millimeter-accurate plumb, level, and structural anchors.

02

Cut & Cold-Forge

Certified prime IS 2062 mild steel, SS 304, or aluminium sections are precision-sliced on industrial bandsaws and shaped using hydraulic presses and our specialized Ludhiana forge.

03

TIG/MIG Weld & Hand Finish

Full penetration welds ensure lifetime structural integrity. Our master craftsmen blend every weld seam smooth with multi-grit flap discs, apply anti-corrosion zinc primer, and finish with electrostatically bonded powder coat or PU paint.

04

White-Glove Installation

Our dedicated fabrication crew installs and bolts the finished structure on-site using heavy-duty chemical anchors. Flawless alignments, zero vibration, smooth operation, guaranteed.
